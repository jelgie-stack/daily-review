# Conversion drop (ecommerce profile)

Sessions were normal; fewer of them bought.

1. **Site health.** Error rates, page speed, checkout failures, payment-provider incidents,
   a deploy yesterday. Ask engineering before marketing.
2. **Availability.** Out-of-stock on hero products, shipping cutoffs, a delivery-estimate
   change at checkout.
3. **Traffic quality.** The same session count from a different source (a viral post, a bot
   spike, a new campaign on a cold audience) converts worse. Break sessions down by source.
4. **Price and promotion.** A promotion ending drops conversion sharply for a day or two.
5. **Device and geography.** A drop confined to one device type or region points to a
   technical cause.

## Deciding

Conversion drops are the one flag worth escalating the same morning, because the cause is
often a defect that costs money every hour it stands.
