# New-customer drop: fewer first-time buyers

First-time buyers are the most marketing-sensitive number in the brief, so check marketing
before anything else.

1. **Spend.** Paid media spend by platform by day for the last 7 days. A budget cap, a paused
   campaign, a card decline, or a learning-phase reset shows up here within a day.
2. **Delivery, not spend.** Impressions and clicks by day. Spend steady with impressions down
   means an auction change or a disapproved ad.
3. **Tracking.** If clicks are steady and first orders fell, suspect attribution before demand:
   a tag broke, a consent banner changed, a new-customer definition changed in the warehouse.
4. **Definition drift.** How is "new" defined (first order ever, first in 12 months, first on
   this channel)? If the definition changed upstream the number is not comparable.
5. **Mix.** If new customers fell only in one channel or unit, it inherits that channel's or
   unit's playbook.

## Deciding

A new-customer drop with steady spend and steady clicks is a tracking question first. A drop
that matches a spend drop is a budget question and usually fixable the same day.
