# General playbook

Use this when a flag has no playbook of its own, or when revenue moved and the brief could not
say why. Work down the list and stop at the first check that explains the move.

1. **Data first.** Did every unit report? Compare the count of units with revenue yesterday to
   the count a week ago. A missing feed looks exactly like a sales collapse. Check for late-
   arriving rows: rerun the review in the afternoon if the warehouse loads in batches.
2. **Calendar.** Holidays, local events, school schedules, sports. The method compares to the
   same weekday, so a holiday Thursday is judged against normal Thursdays and will look wrong.
3. **Weather.** Pull yesterday's weather for the markets that moved. Rain and snow move
   foot-traffic businesses by 5 to 20% and nothing else needs to be true.
4. **Promotions and pricing.** Did a promotion start, end, or change yesterday? Did prices
   change? Both show up as a value (AOV) move first.
5. **Marketing.** Paid media spend by day, email and push sends, and anything that was turned
   off or capped. A budget cap hit on a Wednesday shows up as fewer new customers on Thursday.
6. **Operations.** Outages, closures, staffing, supply. Ask the people who run the units.
7. **Competition.** New openings nearby, a competitor promotion. Slow-moving but real.

If none of these explains it, write that down. "Unexplained, watching" is a legitimate verdict
and better than a story invented to fill the gap.
