# Channel shift: revenue moved between channels

A mix shift is only a problem if it costs margin or hides a loss. Decide which it is.

1. **Net loss or pure mix?** Add the channels up. If total revenue is on baseline and share
   moved, it is mix. If one channel fell and nothing rose, it is a loss wearing a mix costume;
   go to `volume_drop.md` for that channel.
2. **Cause of a mix shift.** The usual ones:
   - a third-party marketplace promotion (delivery share jumps, margin falls),
   - an app or site outage (online falls, in-store rises),
   - weather (delivery rises on bad days),
   - a channel-specific discount.
3. **Margin.** If the channel that grew carries lower margin (marketplace fees, delivery
   commissions), say so: "revenue flat, contribution down". That is the line an executive needs.
4. **Persistence.** A one-day shift is weather or a promo. A shift that holds for a week is a
   customer behaviour change and deserves a plan.
