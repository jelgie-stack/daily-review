# Daypart shift (restaurant profile)

A daypart moved share while the top line may or may not have.

1. **Which daypart, which direction.** Lunch down with dinner flat: weather, nearby workplace
   patterns, a competitor lunch deal. Dinner down: staffing, wait times, competition, events.
   Breakfast or afternoon moves are small in dollars and rarely worth a line unless persistent.
2. **Hours.** A unit that opened late or closed early moves daypart share with nothing else
   changing. Check against the unit table.
3. **Promotions by daypart.** Happy-hour or lunch-combo offers starting or ending.
4. **Persistence.** Dayparts are noisy. Require two days before assigning anything.
